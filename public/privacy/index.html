Privacy policy

Telar Clips is run by Telar Labs. This page explains what information is collected when you visit.

No accounts

You can browse and download every clip without creating an account. We don't ask for your name, email or payment details to download.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalized ads are used.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) to count page views. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't track you across websites.

Hosting and logs

The site and clips are hosted on Cloudflare. Like any website, their servers may record basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the service secure and working.

Emails

If you email us, we use your email address only to reply to you.

Children

The site is for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about privacy: telarlabs@gmail.com
