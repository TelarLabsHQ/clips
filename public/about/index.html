About Telar Clips

Telar Clips is a small library of free motion backgrounds, overlays and green screen clips made by Telar Labs, an independent studio.

We started it because creators, teachers, churches and small businesses everywhere keep needing the same things: red lanterns for a Lunar New Year greeting, a full moon for Chuseok, falling sakura for a spring post, bats for a Halloween invite, a parol for a Filipino Christmas, confetti for a giveaway winner, a calm loop behind a quote. The big stock sites have millions of clips, but the good holiday ones are often hard to find or need a paid plan.

Every clip here is an original motion graphic we designed and rendered ourselves. They are free to download without an account, and you can use them in personal and commercial projects. See the license for the details.

What you'll find

  • World Celebrations: Holidays from around the world: Lunar New Year lanterns, the Chuseok and Mid-Autumn moon, Japanese cherry blossoms, Halloween, the Fourth of July, the Filipino parol and festival bunting.
  • Christmas & New Year: Snow, string lights, ornaments, fireworks and a New Year countdown for your holiday greetings and promos.
  • Green Screen: Pure chroma green (#00B140) clips you can drop over any video and key out in CapCut, Canva, Premiere or DaVinci Resolve.
  • Bokeh Lights: Soft out-of-focus lights in many colors, perfect behind titles, quotes and greetings.
  • Gradients: Smooth, slowly flowing color gradients for intros, presentations and social posts.
  • Overlays: Light rays, light leaks, sparkles, snow, dust and embers to lay over your footage with Screen blend mode.
  • Celebrations & Events: Confetti, balloons, hearts and graduation colors for birthdays, weddings, debuts and awards.
  • Nature & Seasons: Autumn leaves, fireflies, bubbles and starry skies for seasonal greetings, relaxing, sleep, kids and storytelling videos.
  • Tech & Abstract: Network lines, hexagons and retro neon grids for tech, gaming and corporate videos.

New clips every week

We add new clips regularly, especially before holidays like Lunar New Year, Valentine's Day, cherry blossom season, Chuseok, Halloween, Christmas and New Year. Have an idea for a clip you need? Tell us, and we may make it.

How the site is supported

Telar Clips is free because it shows ads. The ads help pay for hosting and the time it takes to make new clips.
