How to Make a Birthday Greeting Video in 1 Minute

A short birthday video feels more personal than a text message, and it doesn't have to take long. With a free moving background, a name and a few words, you can make one in about a minute.

What you need

  • A phone or computer with CapCut, Canva, or any video editor.
  • A moving background. Balloons, confetti or warm bokeh lights work well. Pick one from the list at the end of this page.
  • The person's name, and one or two short lines.

The 1-minute method

  1. Pick your background. Rising balloons feel playful, gold bokeh feels elegant, and confetti feels like a party. Download it free; there's no sign-up.
  2. Start a project in your editor and add the background as the first layer. For TikTok, Reels or WhatsApp Status, set the project to 9:16 and zoom the clip to fill the screen.
  3. Add the name in big letters. For example: Happy 7th Birthday, Mia! A rounded or script font looks friendly. Make it large and place it in the upper half.
  4. Add a short message underneath in a smaller, simple font: Wishing you a year full of laughter. Love, Tita Grace.
  5. Make the text easy to read. Use white or cream text with a soft shadow, or a light outline. Avoid thin fonts on busy backgrounds.
  6. Add a gentle animation to the text, such as "fade in" or "pop", so it appears after half a second.
  7. Set the length to 8 to 15 seconds. All our clips loop, so if you need it longer, copy the background and paste it end to end. You won't see the join.
  8. Export at 1080p and send it.

Make it extra special

  • Add a photo. Place a photo of the birthday person in a rounded frame in the middle, with the name above it.
  • Add confetti on top. Lay a confetti green screen over the whole video for a burst of celebration. See how to use a green screen in CapCut.
  • Record a short voice message. Most editors let you record straight into the project. Even "Happy birthday, we love you!" makes it personal.
  • Count the age. A big number like "7" or "50" that appears first, then the name, is a fun reveal.

Wording ideas

  • Happy birthday, Lolo! Thank you for every story and every hug.
  • Happy 18th, Sam! The world is yours.
  • To the best teacher: happy birthday, Ms. Lopez!
  • Happy birthday from all of us at the office!

A note about music

Music makes a greeting feel complete, but not every song is free to use. If you'll post the video publicly, use the music library inside TikTok, Instagram or CapCut, or a track marked as free to use. For a private message to family, any song you like is usually fine.

Other greetings you can make the same way

The same steps work for graduations, anniversaries, Christmas, New Year and fiestas. Browse our celebration backgrounds and Christmas and New Year backgrounds for more ideas.
