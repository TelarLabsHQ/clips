How to make a greeting video

  1. Pick the occasion. Christmas, Pasko, New Year, birthday, thank you, congratulations or love. It fills in a greeting you can change.
  2. Choose a background. Moving snow, lights, fireworks, balloons, hearts and more, from the Telar Clips library.
  3. Type your message and who it’s from.
  4. Style it: font, color and where the text sits.
  5. Pick a shape and length, then tap Download MP4. The video is made on your phone or computer in a few seconds, and nothing is uploaded.

Which shape should I choose?

  • Portrait 9:16 for Facebook and Instagram Stories, TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp Status.
  • Square 1:1 for Facebook and Instagram posts and group chats.
  • Landscape 16:9 for YouTube, a TV or projector at a party, or a church or school screen.

More on sizes in our guide to video sizes for TikTok, Reels, Shorts and YouTube.

Tips

  • Keep it short. A greeting of 3 to 8 words, and one sentence of message, reads best on a phone.
  • Add music in TikTok, Instagram or CapCut after downloading, using their licensed music.
  • Making many? Your last design is remembered on this device, so you only change the name and download again.
  • Want your own text style? Download the background itself from its clip page and use any video editor. See how to make a holiday greeting video.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no watermark you can’t remove. The small “Made with Telar Clips” note is optional.

Can I use it for my business or church?

Yes. The backgrounds are free for personal and commercial use under the Telar Clips license.

It doesn’t work on my phone.

Video making needs a recent browser. Chrome, Edge and Safari work best. If a long video fails, try 8 or 15 seconds.
