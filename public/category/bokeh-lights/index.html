What bokeh is good for

Bokeh is the soft, round glow of lights out of focus. It looks warm and festive without showing anything specific, so it suits almost any celebration and keeps attention on your words.

  • Greetings for birthdays, anniversaries, Christmas, Valentine’s Day and graduations.
  • Quote and verse videos, where a calm, glowing background sets the mood.
  • Event screens for weddings, debuts, awards nights and church services.

Picking a color

  • Gold and warm white for formal and holiday events.
  • Pink and red for Valentine’s Day, weddings and debuts.
  • Blue, teal and purple for calm, modern or evening themes.
  • Navy and gold for graduations and school events.

Tips

  • Place a photo over bokeh with a soft shadow or white border so it stands out from the background.
  • Lower the clip’s brightness a little in your editor if your text is light-colored.
  • Bokeh loops are seamless, so one clip can run as long as you need behind a slideshow.
