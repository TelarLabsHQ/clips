Ideas for these clips

  • Christmas greetings: put your family photo or a short message over falling snow or string lights, and send it in your group chat.
  • Church and school programs: play a slow loop on the screen while people arrive, then switch to the program title.
  • New Year countdowns: use a fireworks or countdown clip for the last minute of a party livestream or a New Year post.
  • Business promos: holiday hours, gift ideas and year-end sales look more festive with a gentle moving background.

Tips for a clean result

  • Keep text short and in the middle of the screen. Snowflakes and lights move behind it, so a thin dark box under the words helps them stay readable.
  • Gold, white and deep red text work well on most of these backgrounds.
  • Most clips are 8-second seamless loops. For a 30-second greeting, place the clip four times in a row in your editor; the joins won’t show.
  • Add your own music. The clips have no sound, so they work with any song or narration.

Most clips are Full HD (1920 × 1080) MP4 files with no sound, and many are seamless loops. They are free for personal and commercial use; see the license.
