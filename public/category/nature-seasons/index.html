Ideas

  • Seasonal greetings: autumn leaves for Thanksgiving or a fall sale, a starry sky for a goodnight message.
  • Kids’ and storytelling videos: fireflies and bubbles as a calm background behind a story or a lesson.
  • Relaxing and sleep videos: loop a calm clip for several minutes with soft music.
  • Prayer and reflection: a quiet background for a verse, a prayer or a moment of silence.

Making a long, calm video

These clips loop seamlessly, so you can repeat one for 10 minutes or an hour without a visible jump. In CapCut, add the clip, then duplicate it until the timeline is long enough. Add music last, and fade it in and out.

Tips

  • For bedtime and relaxing videos, slow the clip down to 0.75× and lower the brightness a little.
  • Keep text minimal. A single line for 5 seconds at the start is usually enough.

Most clips are Full HD (1920 × 1080) MP4 files with no sound, and many are seamless loops. They are free for personal and commercial use; see the license.
