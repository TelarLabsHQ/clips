How overlays work

Overlays are effects on a black background, like light rays, sparkles, snow, dust or embers. You put them on top of your own video or photo, then change the blend mode so the black disappears and only the light stays.

In CapCut

  1. Add your main video or photo first.
  2. Tap Overlay → Add overlay and choose the overlay clip.
  3. Stretch it to fill the screen, then tap Blend → Screen.
  4. Lower the opacity if the effect is too strong.

In Canva

Add the overlay above your photo or video, stretch it to fill the page, and set its transparency lower so the picture shows through. Screen blending gives the cleanest result in editors that offer it, like CapCut, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve.

Ideas

  • Light leaks and rays to make phone footage of a wedding or debut look warm and cinematic.
  • Snow over an outdoor video for a Christmas greeting.
  • Sparkles for a reveal: a new product, a baby announcement or a winner.
  • Rain or dust for a calm, moody storytelling scene.
