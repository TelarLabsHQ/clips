Ideas

  • Birthdays and debuts: confetti or balloons behind the name and age, or as the opening of a slideshow.
  • Weddings and anniversaries: floating hearts behind the couple’s names and the date.
  • Graduations and awards: navy and gold for school colors, confetti for the moment each name is called.
  • Office and church events: a loop on the screen as people arrive, with the event title on top.

Making a one-minute greeting

  1. Start with a celebration clip and the person’s name, 3 to 5 seconds.
  2. Add 6 to 10 photos, about 4 seconds each, with the clip playing behind them.
  3. End with a short message from the group and the date.

Our guide to a birthday greeting video in 1 minute walks through it in CapCut.

Tips

  • Use bold, rounded fonts for birthdays and kids’ events, and simple serif fonts for weddings and formal awards.
  • Keep the person’s photos large and the text short; the moving background should frame the moment, not compete with it.
