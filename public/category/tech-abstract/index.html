Ideas

  • Tech and gaming intros: neon grids and network lines behind a channel name or game title.
  • Corporate videos: a blue hexagon or particle background for product updates, training videos and conference screens.
  • School presentations: science, robotics and ICT topics look modern with a moving network background.
  • Livestream screens: “Starting soon” and “Be right back” screens for streams and webinars.

Tips

  • These backgrounds are busy, so give text a solid or semi-transparent box behind it.
  • Cool blues pair well with white and light-cyan text; the retro neon grid works best with bold pink, purple or yellow titles.
  • Our guide to a “Starting soon” screen for your livestream shows how to build one in a few minutes.
