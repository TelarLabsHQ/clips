Quick answers

Can I use it for TikTok or Reels?
Yes. Set your project to 9:16 and zoom the clip to fill the frame. Choose the part of the scene you like best; the motion still loops.
How do I make it longer?
Copy the clip and place the copies end to end. Each one is 8 seconds and the end matches the start, so 8 copies make a smooth background of about one minute with no visible jump.
Does it have sound?
No. It is silent so you can add your own music or voice.
