Free to copy and change. This clip will sit on top of your video, so keep text in the area the effect leaves open.

How to use this green screen clip

  1. CapCut (phone or desktop)

    Add your main video first. Tap Overlay → Add overlay and choose this clip. Select it, tap Remove BG → Chroma key, pick the green, then raise Intensity until the green disappears and Shadow to clean the edges.

  2. Canva

    Place your background or video, then add this clip on top. Select it and choose Edit → BG Remover (Canva Pro). For a free option, use CapCut instead.

  3. Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve

    Put the clip on the track above your footage. In Premiere use Ultra Key and pick the green; in Resolve use the 3D Keyer or Delta Keyer.

Quick answers

Is there a vertical version?
Yes. Christmas Lights Frame Green Screen (Vertical 9:16) is the same design made for TikTok, Reels and Shorts (9:16).
How do I make it longer?
Copy the clip and place the copies end to end. Each one is 8 seconds and the end matches the start, so 8 copies make a smooth background of about one minute with no visible jump.
Which green is it?
Chroma green #00B140. Pick it with the eyedropper in your editor’s chroma key tool.
