Fiesta Banderitas Green Screen

Colorful banderitas (bunting flags) flutter across the top of the frame. Pure chroma green (#00B140) for easy removal in CapCut, Canva, Premiere or DaVinci Resolve. 8-second seamless loop, 1920x1080, 30 fps. Motion graphic by Telar Labs.

Good for

Add a fiesta border to any video: barangay events, school programs, party reels.

How to use this green screen clip

  1. CapCut (phone or desktop)

    Add your main video first. Tap Overlay → Add overlay and choose this clip. Select it, tap Remove BG → Chroma key, pick the green, then raise Intensity until the green disappears and Shadow to clean the edges.

  2. Canva

    Place your background or video, then add this clip on top. Select it and choose Edit → BG Remover (Canva Pro). For a free option, use CapCut instead.

  3. Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve

    Put the clip on the track above your footage. In Premiere use Ultra Key and pick the green; in Resolve use the 3D Keyer or Delta Keyer.

More tips in our how-to-use guide.
