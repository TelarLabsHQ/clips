Bright confetti in red, yellow, green, blue and purple tumbles and falls over a dark background. Seamless 8-second loop, 1920x1080, 30 fps. Motion graphic by Telar Labs.

Good for

Birthdays, graduations, product launches, giveaways and winner announcements.

How to use this background

  1. Put it on the bottom track

    Add the clip as the first layer of your video. It is a seamless loop, so you can repeat it as many times as you need without a visible jump.

  2. Add your text or photo on top

    Type your greeting, quote, title or announcement over it. Light text with a soft shadow reads best on darker backgrounds.

  3. Make it vertical if needed

    For TikTok, Reels or Shorts, set the project to 9:16 and zoom the clip to fill the frame. The motion still loops smoothly.
