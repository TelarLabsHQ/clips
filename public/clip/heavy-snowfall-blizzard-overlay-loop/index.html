How to use this overlay

  1. Put it above your video

    Place this clip on the track above your footage or photo, stretched to the full frame.

  2. Set the blend mode to Screen

    The black parts disappear and only the light stays. CapCut: select the overlay → Blend → Screen. Premiere: Effect Controls → Opacity → Blend mode → Screen. Canva: Transparency works, but Screen blend needs CapCut or a desktop editor.

  3. Adjust the strength

    Lower the overlay opacity to 40–80% for a subtle look, or keep it at 100% for a bold effect.

Quick answers

Can I use it for TikTok or Reels?
Yes. Set your project to 9:16 and zoom the clip to fill the frame. The edges will be cropped, so keep your subject near the center.
How do I make it longer?
Copy the clip and place the copies end to end. Each one is 8 seconds and the end matches the start, so 8 copies make a smooth background of about one minute with no visible jump.
Why is the background black?
Black disappears with the Screen blend mode, leaving only the light. That keeps the file small and the edges soft.
