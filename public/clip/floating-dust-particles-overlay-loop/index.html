Floating Dust Particles Overlay

Download free MP4

Soft dust particles float slowly in the air, some sharp and some out of focus. Made on black so you can lay it over your own video with Screen blend mode. Seamless 8-second loop, 1920x1080, 30 fps. Original motion graphic by Telar Labs.

Good for

Cinematic edits, documentaries, product shots, dreamy scenes.

How to use this overlay

  1. Put it above your video

    Place this clip on the track above your footage or photo, stretched to the full frame.

  2. Set the blend mode to Screen

    The black parts disappear and only the light stays. CapCut: select the overlay → Blend → Screen. Premiere: Effect Controls → Opacity → Blend mode → Screen. Canva: Transparency works, but Screen blend needs CapCut or a desktop editor.

  3. Adjust the strength

    Lower the overlay opacity to 40–80% for a subtle look, or keep it at 100% for a bold effect.
