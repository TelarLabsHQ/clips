License

All clips on Telar Clips are free to use in personal and commercial projects. No sign-up, no payment and no credit required.

You can

  • Use the clips in videos on YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and other platforms, including monetized channels.
  • Use them in ads, product videos, presentations, school projects, church services and events.
  • Edit them: cut, crop, recolor, speed up, add text, combine with other footage.
  • Use them in client work you are paid for.

You can't

  • Sell, re-upload or share the clips on their own, or as part of a stock, template or wallpaper collection, without major changes.
  • Claim the clips as your own original work, or register them with a content ID system.
  • Use the clips in a trademark, logo or brand identity.
  • Use them in content that is illegal, hateful, or misleading.

Credit

Credit is not required, but it helps us a lot. If you'd like to, write: Background by Telar Clips.

Common questions

Can I use a clip in a monetized YouTube or Facebook video?

Yes. Using a clip as part of your own video, with your own words, photos, music or footage, is allowed, including on monetized channels.

Why can’t I register the clips with Content ID?

Because other people use the same clips. If one person registers a clip, everyone else’s videos can get false copyright claims. If you receive a claim on a video that uses a Telar Clips clip, contact us with the link and we’ll help.

Can I use the clips in a template I sell?

Not on their own. Selling a template, pack or wallpaper collection where the clip is the main part would be reselling the clip. Using a clip inside a finished video you make for a client is fine.

Can I edit the clips?

Yes. Recolor, crop, slow down, combine and add text as much as you like.

Do I have to give credit?

No. If you’d like to, a line in the description like Background by Telar Clips is enough.
